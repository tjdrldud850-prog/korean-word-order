Korean word order is flexible in speech, but the verb almost always comes last. If your order is natural but not the basic one, you'll see the basic order too. In Hard mode the little particles (는, 를, 에서…) are separate cards: they stick to the word right before them. Intermediate gives you two sentences at once, with particles split. Each sentence ends with its own verb.
